Rules

Splendor: Sandwich Edition
 Objective: Be the first player to earn 15 Sandwich Points by acquiring ingredient sources, completing recipes, and making sandwiches.
Ingredient Tokens:
Lettuce (Green)
Tomatoes (Red)
Nuts (Black)
Jars of Mayonnaise (White)
Cheese (Blue)
Bread (Gold, wild token)
Investment Cards:
Base Sources (Level 1): Simple, low-cost investments (e.g., Tomato field).
Premium Sources (Level 2/3): Fancier, higher-point investments (e.g., Mayonnaise factory).
Sandwiches:
Having a steady source of certain ingredients (e.g. 4 lettuce, 4 cheese) allows you to make specific sandwiches, earning you bonus points
Gameplay
On your turn, choose ONE of these actions:
1. Gather Ingredients
Take 3 different ingredient tokens (e.g., 1 Lettuce, 1 Tomato, 1 Cheese). OR Take 2 identical tokens (only if at least 4 remain in that pile). Bread Rule: You may reserve a card (see below) to take 1 Bread (wild token).
2. Buy an Investment Card
Pay the ingredient cost shown on the card (e.g., 2 Tomatoes + 1 Cheese) to add it to your collection. Cards give permanent discounts (e.g., a Tomato card means future Tomato costs are reduced by 1). Cards may also award Panos Points (1–5).
3. Reserve a Card (+ Take Bread)
Take 1 face-up or random card from the deck and reserve it, blocking any other player from buying it Gain 1 Bread token (wild) as a bonus.
4. Craft a sandwich
If you meet a sandwiches ingredient requirements (e.g., "4 Mayo + 4 Cheese"), claim them immediately!
Each awards 3 Panos Points and is kept until game end.
Winning the Game
The first player to reach 15 Panos Points triggers the final round. Finish the round so all players have equal turns. Highest points wins! Ties go to the player with the fewest Investment Cards.
